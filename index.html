Ball Crusher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

Ball Crusher does not collect, share, or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your game progress (money, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad tracking
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools
  • No internet connection is needed to play

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
