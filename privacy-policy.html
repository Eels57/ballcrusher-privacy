Ball Crusher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Summary

Ball Crusher does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information ourselves. Your game progress is saved only on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob, which are described below.

Game data

Your progress (money, upgrades, skins, settings) is stored on your own device and is never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ball Crusher uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads. For example, you can choose to watch a video for a bonus spin or bonus earnings. When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, if you allow tracking on iOS
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model and operating system version
  • Which ads were shown and how you interacted with them
  • Crash logs and performance data from the ads software

Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and (if you allow it) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive this information from Google in a form that identifies you. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission (iOS): the first time you open the game, iOS may ask whether the app can track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • Consent (Europe, UK, Switzerland and some other regions): where required by law, you will be shown a consent form before any ads are requested. You can change your choice when the form is offered again.
  • Ads are optional: you can play the whole game without watching any video.

Children

Ball Crusher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
